Potential Materials:

  • A phone or laptop
  • Spotify, Apple music, Youtube or a streaming platform of your choice
  • Headphones
  • 20-30min
  • Method to make note of songs

Instructions:

  1. Choose a time of day when you are alone or want to reset your mood.
  2. Pick one feeling you want the playlist to create. Examples: calm, confident, nostalgic, focused, lonely, excited, or peaceful.
  3. Find one song that immediately matches that feeling.
  4. Listen to the song once without doing anything else.
  5. Choose three more songs that feel similar because of their lyrics, beat, voice, or memories.
  6. Add one unexpected song that does not completely fit, but makes the playlist more personal.
  7. Arrange the songs in an order that feels like a small emotional story:
    beginning, build-up, peak, and ending.
  8. Create a title using a short phrase, a memory, a place, or a time of day.
  9. Listen to the playlist from beginning to end.
  10. Do not skip any song. Notice which song changes the mood the most.

    11. At the end you should now have a short playlist you can return to whenever you would like to reset your mood
