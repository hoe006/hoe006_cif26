Mid-term reflection

I’ve noticed that all of my web pages have some level of interactivity, even when the project doesn’t require it. For example, my “Walk” project was about documenting a walk, but I still found ways to make the experience interactive through navigation and exploration. Rather than having someone just read or look at the page, I like giving them something to do and a reason to explore further. While interaction connects my projects, the visual style changes with each one. I’m still exploring different approaches and figuring out what works best for each project’s requirements instead of sticking to one consistent style. Each project gives me a chance to try something different and see how those choices affect the overall experience. So far, I’ve learned that I’m interested in how people move through a website, not just how it looks, and that I want the interaction and visual design to feel connected. Next, I want to experiment more with layout and style, especially by trying compositions and typographic choices I wouldn’t normally use. I’m curious about working with unexpected placement, different scales, and more open space rather than relying on arrangements that already feel familiar.
